Blinded thread rating

60 threads from an online asthma community. Enter the password you were given and your initials. Your ratings are saved in this browser as you go; export them as a CSV when you finish.

Threads
Rate the thread as a whole (opening post plus the replies shown), as if a community member came across it today. Judge the content, not the writing or the length. Judge as of today, not the posting date.
1. Is the topic still of interest to community members today?
2. Would this thread be useful to a community member reading it today?
Lower the score by one level for advice that is clearly outdated or possibly unsafe today; score 0 if that advice is the main content. Do not lower it when another reply corrects it, when it is framed as not being advice, or when it is a dated side remark in a thread whose main content still holds.
3. Optional: a questionable piece of advice that another reply corrects?
4. Do you remember having seen this thread before?
5. Comment (optional)
Instructions

Rate alone, in one or more sittings, without discussing threads with the other raters until all forms are returned. Threads are anonymised and shuffled; dates, view counts and usernames have been removed. Do not try to work out when a thread was posted or how much it was read.

Every answer is saved in this browser automatically when you press "Save and next" or move to another thread. When all 60 are done, press "Export CSV" and send the file back. If you change computer or browser, export first: the saved answers do not follow you.
